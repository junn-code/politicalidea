THE FEDERAL CONSTITUTION

Yang di-Pertuan Agong

The constitutional monarch and Head of State of Malaysia.

Official portrait 01 / 02
01

Constitutional position

The Yang di-Pertuan Agong is Malaysia's constitutional monarch and Head of State. The office is established under Article 32 of the Federal Constitution.

02

Election and term

The Yang di-Pertuan Agong is elected by the Conference of Rulers from among the Malay Rulers. The term of office is five years, subject to the Constitution.

03

Constitutional functions

The office carries out constitutional functions, including appointing the Prime Minister in accordance with the Constitution and performing formal functions in Parliament.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Independent. Informative. Transparent.

Political Compass is designed as an educational platform for exploring political ideas and public information in Malaysia.

Political neutrality

The platform does not promote a political party, candidate or ideology.

Source transparency

Political claims and policy positions should be linked to identifiable sources and dates.

Room for complexity

Political views can be mixed, uncertain or changing. A single label cannot describe every person.

The political test is an educational self-reflection tool. Its dimensions and scoring model are simplified and should not be treated as a validated psychological or scientific instrument.
